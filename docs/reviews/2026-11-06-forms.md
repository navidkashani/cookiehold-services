# Forms and embedded chat review — 2026-11-06

Ten permanent IDs reviewed: eight corrected and two unresolved. Corrections
are scoped below; this is not a claim that every field is fully verified.

## Scope and consumer impact

Reviewed purpose, provider, privacy URL, locations, cookies/storage, address
patterns, Consent Mode and WordPress hints. Categories, legal bases,
placeholders, Consent Mode flags, addresses, inline matches and WordPress
hints are unchanged. A vendor's own consent feature or Google integration
does not establish a CookieHold integration or a site's legal requirements.

Two exact chat-UI cookie names are added to `hubspot-forms`. Removing them
can reset the open/closed state and redisplay a dismissed welcome message.
They do not overlap other catalog cookies. Consent-preference,
authentication and shared CDN cookies are not added to broad cleanup rules.
No translations are generated. Revised location fields omit DPF labels;
that is not a finding that a vendor withdrew from the program.

Author-distributed WordPress entry files were fetched on the review date:
[HubSpot 11.3.75](https://plugins.svn.wordpress.org/leadin/trunk/leadin.php),
[Typeform 2.9.1](https://plugins.svn.wordpress.org/typeform/trunk/index.php),
[Jotform 1.4.0](https://plugins.svn.wordpress.org/embed-form/trunk/jotform-wp-embed.php),
[Chatbase 1.0.4](https://plugins.svn.wordpress.org/chatbase/trunk/chatbase.php),
[SalesIQ 2.0.5](https://plugins.svn.wordpress.org/zoho-salesiq/trunk/index.php)
and [Zoho Forms 5.0.1](https://plugins.svn.wordpress.org/zoho-forms/trunk/zohoForms.php).
These establish file identity and, where present, emitted snippets; they are
not installation, browser or CookieHold compatibility tests. No extra
generic script handles are added.

## hubspot

Outcome: **corrected**, version 1 → 2, with unresolved findings.

The [hosting FAQ](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/account-security/hubspot-cloud-infrastructure-and-data-hosting-frequently-asked-questions)
lists account hosting in the USA, EU, Canada and Australia, plus processing
exceptions outside the account region. Replace the USA-only location with
those account regions and other-country processing. The
[cookie reference](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/privacy-and-consent/what-cookies-does-hubspot-set-in-a-visitor-s-browser)
supports all four existing analytics lifetimes. No new guidance.

Unresolved: the cookie page also mixes HubSpot Product storage, hosted-site
authentication and consent preferences with visitor tracking. Establish
which storage belongs to third-party tracking embeds before adding it.
The [regional terms](https://legal.hubspot.com/jst-europe) exclude several
European customer countries from the Ireland contract. Reconcile the
privacy-policy recipient with the applicable contracting entity before
replacing the existing provider globally. No provider change is claimed.

the USA, plus other operational providers. Replace Belgium-only with EU
hosting and feature-dependent USA/other-country processing. Do not confuse
each processor's access with access to form submissions: the table makes
that distinction explicitly.

Guidance points out the default answer content in
[self email notifications](https://tally.so/help/self-email-notifications)
and the customization option. Unresolved: the current
[widget script](https://tally.so/widgets/embed.js) returned HTTP 403 during
this review. Recheck the two existing popup storage prefixes in a delivered
widget before changing them. Empty cookies are not a guarantee that every
optional payment, upload or bot-protection flow is storage-free.
