# Media review: 2026-11-06

Ten entries reviewed. Seven receive supported location/source corrections;
three remain unresolved without runtime changes. Every entry has remaining
questions, listed below. This is a documentation review, not a browser test
or a claim of tested WordPress compatibility.

## Scope and impact

All ten retain their permanent ids, purpose, provider, privacy URL, category,
legal basis, matching patterns, cookies, storage keys, Consent Mode flags,
placeholder and WordPress hints. The catalog policy fields do not establish
legal requirements. Empty storage lists do not mean storage is absent.
None is a Google tag merely because its vendor website uses Google tools.

Changed entries advance one version and record the review date. Locations
describe the vendor's stated processing scope, not a guarantee of where a
particular site's visitor data stays. Removing a transfer-framework label
from a location is not a finding that certification was lost. The generic
registry links in existing sources are insufficient to renew certification
claims without a readable entity-specific record.

No translations were generated or changed in this batch. Six services gain
English guidance with note-level sources and verification dates; the build
keeps it outside the plugin download index.

## vimeo

Outcome: **corrected**, version 2 → 3, with unresolved findings.

- [Privacy policy](https://vimeo.com/legal/privacy/policy), updated October 7,
  2026, identifies Vimeo.com, Inc. and processing in the USA and other
  countries. Replace `USA (EU-US Data Privacy Framework)` with `USA and other countries`.
- [Player cookies](https://help.vimeo.com/hc/en-us/articles/26080940921361-Vimeo-Player-Cookies)
  supports the five existing names and lifetimes. It also describes other
  conditional cookies; the catalog is not a complete inventory for every
  embed. Do not claim shared Cloudflare names exclusively for Vimeo.
- [Player parameters](https://help.vimeo.com/hc/en-us/articles/12426260232977-About-Player-Parameters)
  supports the player host and the optional `dnt` setting. Add it to sources.
  Guidance covers enabling the option and the limits involving essential
  and previously stored cookies.

Unresolved: exact local-storage keys and conditional player cookies need
vendor documentation or an observed, reproducible player trace. Dynamic
prefixes before fixed cookie-name suffixes cannot be represented safely by
the catalog's trailing-wildcard syntax; do not broaden matches to compensate.

## apple-music

Outcome: **corrected**, version 1 → 2, with unresolved findings.

Apple Distribution International Limited for EEA personal data and describes
general US storage plus access and processing worldwide. Change the location
from `USA` to `USA and other countries`.

[Web-player privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-music-web/)

Daily identifiers and Apple's stated data-retention periods are not evidence
of cookie names or cookie lifetimes.

Unresolved: the empty storage lists remain incomplete rather than verified
empty. The embed and MusicKit patterns need separate runtime coverage checks;
the privacy notice does not prove an exhaustive address list. Preserve the
existing patterns and use Apple's current generated embeds/SDK when testing.
